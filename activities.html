Guidance and Information for Parents

🔍

Early Warning Signs

Sudden behavioral shifts, agitation when receiving device notifications, social withdrawal, and dropping academic results may be key indicators of cyberbullying.

  • Attempting to abruptly hide screen activity
  • Withdrawing from social gatherings and friendships
  • Unexplained physical ailments such as headaches and stomach aches
🛡️

Safe Internet Principles at Home

Adopt an empathetic, communicative approach rather than punitive restrictions. Your child should feel secure reaching out to you whenever an issue arises.

  • Establish screen time limits and privacy rules collaboratively
  • Explain personal information protection and password hygiene
  • Guide them never to retaliate directly and to save screenshots as proof
