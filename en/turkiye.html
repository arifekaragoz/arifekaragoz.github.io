🇹🇷 Turkey Partnership Materials

Activities, student-teacher works, and shared educational resources carried out under the Turkish partnership in the Erasmus+ "Digital Empathy" project.

Our Work Within the Project

🏫

Classroom Awareness

Guidance lessons, interactive role-playing, and case analyses were conducted on defining cyberbullying, recognizing its variants, and understanding digital footprints.

✈️

Benevento Mobility

Our delegation of Turkish teachers and students participated in the international project week in Benevento, Italy, conducting joint workshops with Italian, Czech, and Portuguese partners.

🤝

Parent & Community Engagement

Information sessions were organized for parents regarding safe internet practices, screen time management, and early warning signs of cyberbullying.
