Digital Empathy: Raising Awareness and Fostering Cooperation Against Cyberbullying

This project is designed to empower students to exist safely and responsibly in the digital world. Our goal is not only to provide young people with technical digital skills, but also to raise conscious individuals against cyberbullying by strengthening feelings of empathy, respect, and social responsibility. Through interactive activities, family engagement, teacher training, and transnational partnerships, students learn to recognize digital threats, protect themselves, and support others. In this way, a sustainable framework is created for a resilient and empathetic generation equipped with digital citizenship awareness.

The project equips children and youth in the following key domains:

  • 💻 Digital literacy skills
  • 🛡️ Online safety and data privacy
  • ❤️ Empathy, respect, and positive digital communication
  • 🤝 Collaboration and intercultural understanding

🎯 Project Objectives

Beyond acquiring technical digital capabilities, the project aims to:

  • Recognize, prevent, and respond to cyberbullying effectively
  • Cultivate emotional resilience and positive peer relationships
  • Foster respectful and ethical digital habits
  • Build an inclusive and supportive school climate

This project integrates digital transformation and safe internet practices into education through international partnerships.

👥 Target Audience

  • Students aged 10–14 (with particular focus on disadvantaged backgrounds, special educational needs, and migrant students)
  • 👨‍🏫 Teachers (professional development on cyber safety and digital pedagogy)
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Parents (practical guidance on supporting children)
  • 👥 School communities and local educational stakeholders

📊 Why This Project?

Recent studies demonstrate that cyberbullying is on a sharp rise:

  • 27% of students report experiencing cyberbullying firsthand
  • The majority of adolescents do not feel entirely safe online
  • 60% of teachers express a need for professional training in cybersecurity and empathy education
  • Parents increasingly look for clear guidance to address internet-related risks

🧠 How We Implement It

  • 📚 Digital safety educational modules
  • 🎭 Empathy and emotional resilience workshops
  • 🌍 Transnational student mobilities and collaborative activities
  • 🎤 Student-led awareness campaigns
  • 👨‍🏫 Teacher training seminars and peer-learning sessions
  • 👪 Family awareness workshops
