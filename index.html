Digital Empathy: Raising Awareness and Fostering Cooperation Against Cyberbullying

An international Erasmus+ project dedicated to promoting safe, respectful, and empathetic behaviors in digital environments. The project brings together students, teachers, and families to build awareness against cyberbullying, strengthen digital citizenship skills, and foster transnational cooperation.

Our Vision

This project aims to raise awareness and provide educational tools for students, parents, and educators. By understanding the scope and dynamics of cyberbullying, we empower the next generation to act with empathy, resilience, and confidence online.
